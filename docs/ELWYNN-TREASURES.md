# Elwynn — hidden chest ledger

Eighteen authored caches, added 2026-10-16 alongside the three Northshire caches. Each character
can open and claim each cache once. Contents are fixed, saved when opened and granted atomically
when taken; restarting or dying does not refill them. All quantities below are guaranteed.

Six supply caches, four white equipment caches and eight especially concealed green equipment
caches. No map marker or sparkle reveals a closed chest. The same contents apply to every class;
class coverage is spread across the world rather than randomly rolling the player's reward.
An Abbey Salve restores 30 HP. Malachite and Tigerseye are sellable valuables. Item levels and
stats come from the imported vMaNGOS item rows, without unimplemented random-affix bonuses.

All eighteen final placements passed live approach, Open/Take and fixed-reward checks.

classes without enabling unfinished classes or relying on the current permissive fallback in their
equipment rules. The metadata test checks armor type, useful primary stat and required level.

## Implementation and saves

`crates/wow-souls/src/loot/treasures.rs` is the placement/reward source of truth. New IDs are
9000010–9000027; Northshire 9000001–9000003 retain their rewards and claims. Imported baseline
chest rewards/restock rules are unchanged. Some Elwynn sites reuse native hidden `106318` chest
anchors, which the baseline `2843` renderer does not instantiate; do not add a second chest at
these coordinates if expanding imported chest types later.

An authored chest waits for a floor within 1.5 yards of its recorded height, so an unloaded cave
floor cannot redirect it onto surface terrain. Caches whose item metadata is absent from a custom
or smaller world manifest stay hidden rather than awarding an unknown item.
