Built by `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-14-mage-class.md` Task 3.

## What exists

- `wow-souls-dsr casts` writes `cache/dsr/casts.txt` (`casts-version 1`: `cast <role> <clip> <duration> <release> <recover>` and `bullet <role> ...` lines) plus the four clip pairs under `human_male/` and `c0000/`; `extract` runs it as its last step. `wow-souls-dsr casts-survey` lists every clip of `a00_6200-6226` / `6300-6326` / `6400-6426` / `6500-6526` with its flag windows (read-only; output kept in `2026-10-14-mage-casts-survey.txt`).
- Release rule: the release is where `ChrActionFlag` 90 opens, the recovery where 89 opens (the clip's end when it has none). 62xx and 63xx clips have the 91 / 90 / 89 triplets; 64xx and 65xx use the flags differently (89 from 0.00) and are not used.
- `params.rs`: `Magic` and `Bullet` rows (`homingAngle` is an s16).
- Game: `spells::cast::{Role, Timing, Bullet, Kit, parse, load_kit}`; `ClipId::Cast(Role)` and `clips_of(.., casts, ..)`; `WOWSOULS_DSVIEW=fireball|frostbolt|fire_blast|frost_armor` shows a cast beside the DS skeleton.
