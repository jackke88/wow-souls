## Method (same as S2/S3)

New kit = one `Kit` + one `bonemap` pair table in `crates/dsr-cache`, measured against the WoW M2 (`benilla-extract <Data> m2bones <path>`), then `wow-souls-dsr probe --kit <name> --clips ...` for the retarget numbers, then `extract --out cache/dsr-next` (deterministic: the diff against `cache/dsr/manifest.txt` is only the new block) and the new kit folders + manifest copied into `cache/dsr/`. Species row, pattern bands, `world::foe_of`, tests. The remix (`remix.rs`) got a `Rig` per new body so every kit's reactions/deaths/attacks are dressed and tested like the others.

## Spiders (added later the same day; full record in `2026-10-16-handover-spiders-latch.md`)

The first version (dog clips on the spider body) never struck and looked wrong, so the spiders now come from the DS Crag Spider `c3400` through a **pose-relative retarget** (`Pair::rel`: rotations are the DS change from the clip's first frame, since the DS bind is a T-pose). Kits `minespider` (Mine Spider 43, Mother Fang 471) and `tarantula` (Forest Spider 30) are `Tier::Skirmish`: `bite` `a00_3000`, `leap` `a00_3001` (baked into a dodgeable jump with an arc, `bake_leap`), `hop_back` `a00_3500`. A landed leap **latches** (`latch.rs`): the spider hangs at the player's chest for 2 s, chews three times (level 0: no flinch), the player is slowed to 0.45, a roll or a blow shakes it off, and it hops back. Live-verified for Mine Spider and Mother Fang; Forest Spider's leap still misses (open).
